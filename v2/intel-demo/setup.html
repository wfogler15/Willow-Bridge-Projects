Set up submarkets and product types

Willow Bridge classifies properties its own way. The dashboard shows these by default, and anyone can flip to CoStar's version. Nothing changes until you press Save, and you can rerun this anytime.

Willow Bridge standard. Garden: up to 45 units/acre and 4 stories (5 on occasion, surface parked), plus townhomes, single-family and build-to-rent. Mid-Rise: 46–115 units/acre, up to 10 stories (at 5 stories or fewer it needs structured parking, which the density implies). High-Rise: 11+ stories. Density is units ÷ acres from CoStar.
