Set up submarkets and product types

Willow Bridge classifies properties its own way. The dashboard shows these by default, and anyone can flip to CoStar's version. Nothing changes until you press Save, and you can rerun this anytime.

Willow Bridge standard. Garden: up to 51 units/acre and 4 stories (5 on occasion, surface parked), plus townhomes, single-family and build-to-rent. Mid-Rise: up to 10 stories; at 5 stories or fewer it needs structured parking (wrap or podium). High-Rise: 11+ stories. For 3–5 story buildings the page also weighs RealPage parking type, whether surface parking fits on the land, building area and elevators.
